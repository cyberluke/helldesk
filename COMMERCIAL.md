# Commercial licensing — HELLDESK / NAI Lattice

The HELLDESK codebase is released to the public under the
**GNU Affero General Public License v3.0 only** (AGPL-3.0-only) — see
[`LICENSE`](LICENSE). That makes it genuinely free and open source: anyone may
use, study, modify, and redistribute it under the AGPL's terms.

Some organizations cannot or prefer not to operate under the obligations of the
AGPL (notably the network-interaction copyleft of AGPL §13). For those cases, a
**separate commercial license** is available.

## What the commercial license offers

- **Proprietary licensing** of HELLDESK without AGPL copyleft obligations —
  integrate the code closed-source into your own products and services.
- **Enterprise integration** assistance — wiring HELLDESK's per-task engine
  matrix, quality gates, and agent dispatch into your own infrastructure.
- **Customization** — bespoke engine adapters, runners, review pipelines, and
  operator-console workflows.
- **Support** — prioritized engineering support and maintenance agreements.

## What is NOT part of any public or commercial grant

- Customer-specific integration code and private adapters are not published and
  are not part of the public release.
- Credentials, tokens, private keys, and proprietary assets are never licensed
  and never committed.
- Third-party code is governed by its own licenses and is never relicensed
  without authorization.

## Public release self-sufficiency

The public release is independently functional and genuinely open source. The
commercial license is an *addition* for organizations that need AGPL
relief/enterprise services — it does not gate any public functionality.
